Privacy Policyप्राइवेसी पॉलिसी

Last updated: 14 November 2026अंतिम अपडेट: 14 November 2026

Applies to the Gali Bazaar Android app and galibazaar.site.

Gali Bazaar ("we") connects customers with local grocery, food-corner, sweets, fruits & vegetables and stationery shops in their neighbourhood. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Data we collect.

  • Everyone: name and email from your Google account (or your username for shop and sales logins), and the role you use (customer / seller / delivery partner / sales).
  • Customers: mobile number; delivery address (house, block, locality, city, state, PIN, landmark) and the map point of your saved delivery address; orders, chosen delivery slot, chats with the shop, ratings, complaints and complaint photos.
  • Shop owners: shop name, address, map location of the shop, phone, hours, slots, delivery areas, catalogue and product photos, FSSAI (required for food shops), GSTIN (if registered); KYC: PAN number, name on PAN, last 4 digits of Aadhaar, photos of the PAN card and of a masked Aadhaar, and a live selfie; plan payment details (UPI reference / UTR, date, amount, payment screenshot); bills and credit notes. Delivery mode, courier coverage, courier company and tracking numbers; BIS/ISI numbers and product declarations.
  • Delivery partners: name, phone, photo, PAN, last 4 digits of Aadhaar, PAN and masked-Aadhaar photos, live selfie, and UPI payment screenshots they upload.
  • Sales partners: name, phone, email, city; KYC: PAN number, name on PAN, last 4 digits of Aadhaar, photos of the PAN card and of a masked Aadhaar, a live selfie and address; bank account (stored encrypted; only the last 4 digits are shown), IFSC, a cancelled-cheque or passbook photo, UPI ID and GSTIN (if any); shops referred; commission, payouts (with UTR and TDS), Form 16A and the commission rules you accepted.

We never store your full Aadhaar number. We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, call logs or SMS. Map points are worked out from the address you type, so distances shown are approximate. The app shows no third-party ads and uses no advertising trackers.

2. What others can see.

  • The shop and the delivery partner of your order see your name, delivery address and the order, so they can deliver it.
  • The shop and its delivery partner can see your mobile number for your order, only while it is being delivered and for 24 hours after, so they can contact you about it. They may use it only for that order. After that it is shown masked (like XXXXXX3210) in order history, bills and exports.
  • You see the shop's name, address, phone and licence numbers, and the delivery partner's name, photo and phone for your order.
  • For courier orders, the number is also printed on the courier label. The 24 hours start when the parcel is delivered, or when a refused parcel is checked back in at the shop.
  • KYC documents are visible only to Gali Bazaar admins — never to customers, other shops or the sales team.
  • Sales team members see only the name, area and plan status of shops they referred.
  • Shops marked "Sponsored" have paid for a higher position; this label is always shown.

3. Catalogue and Terms records.

  • Product photos and product details uploaded by shops may be used in Gali Bazaar's shared product catalogue so other shops can list the same product.
  • We keep a record of when you accepted our Terms and which version.

4. How we use data. Only to run the service: show shops that deliver to your address, deliver orders, verify shops and delivery partners, send order updates and notifications, handle complaints, make bills and plan invoices, and pay sales commission. We do not sell or rent personal data.

We may send you occasional suggestions about items available at shops near you. You can turn these off in Profile → Notifications.

5. Service providers we use.

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage (India region).
  • Google — Google Sign-In, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and on-device text reading (ML Kit) to check KYC photos; ML Kit runs on your phone.
  • Brevo — emails such as renewal links and sign-in invites.
  • Courier companies — chosen and booked by the shop, not by Gali Bazaar. The shop gives them your name, address and phone number to deliver your courier order.
  • Razorpay IFSC lookup — when a sales partner types an IFSC, only that IFSC code is sent to look up the bank and branch name.
  • UPI apps / banks — when a shop pays for a plan. We receive only the payment reference and screenshot you give.

We may disclose data if the law, a court order or a government authority requires it, or to protect users from fraud.

6. How long we keep data.

  • Account and order data: while your account is active, and up to 90 days after deletion for fraud and dispute handling.
  • Bills, credit notes, payments, commission and TDS records: for as long as tax law requires (generally 8 years).
  • KYC photos: while the shop or delivery partner is active; deleted within 90 days after the account is closed, unless needed for a legal claim.

7. Security. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each user reach only their own data; KYC files are kept in private storage that only admins can read. No system is 100% secure, so please keep your Google account and passwords safe.

8. Your rights. You can see and correct your profile and address in the app, ask for a copy of your data, withdraw consent, delete your account (see Delete account) and raise a grievance with us (see Contact). If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children. Gali Bazaar is for users aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly collect children's data. A parent or guardian who believes a child has used the app can contact us to remove the data.

10. Changes. We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. You will be asked to accept important changes in the app.
