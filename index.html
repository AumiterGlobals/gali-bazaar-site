Help
How do I order?

Install the app, sign in with Google and set your gali. You'll see the shops that deliver to your block. Pick items and a delivery slot, then pay cash or UPI at your door.

How do I install the APK?

Tap Download APK, open the downloaded file and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser once. Got it on WhatsApp as a “.bin” file? Rename it to .apk.

Something wrong with my order?

Open the order in the app and tap Report a problem. The shop must reply within 48 hours.

I own a shop. How do I join?

Install the app, choose Shop owner and finish KYC (PAN + Aadhaar). Your 14-day free trial starts once KYC is approved.

How do I renew my shop plan?

In the app open Account and tap Send renewal link to my email. Open the link to pick a plan and pay.
