Pricing for shops

One small plan. No commission.

Customers always use Gali Bazaar for free. Shops pay one flat plan, and the first 14 days are free.

Already selling? Your plan is renewed from a secure link we email you. In the app, open Shop & KYC and tap Send renewal link to my email.

Install

Install the app in 1 minute

Until the Play Store listing is live, install it directly from here. It's the same app, and updates come to you inside the app.

1

Download

Tap Download the app. Open this page in Chrome for the smoothest download.

2

Allow once

Open the downloaded gali-bazaar.apk. If Android asks, tap Settings → Allow from this source, then go back.

3

Install & sign in

Tap Install, open Gali Bazaar and sign in with Google. Choose Customer or Seller.

Got the app on WhatsApp as a “.bin” file? Rename it to .apk in your file manager, or just download it from this page instead.

Help

Questions

Quick answers. Still stuck? Email support@galibazaar.site

Is the app on Google Play?

The Play Store listing is coming soon. Until then, download the Android app here. It's the same app and updates will come to you.

Is there a delivery charge?

Each shop sets its own delivery charge and "free above" amount. You see it clearly before you place the order.

How do I pay?

Cash or UPI at your door, after checking your items. You never pay inside the app.

What if an item is bad?

Return it at the door and don't pay for it. If you find a problem later, open the order and tap "Report a problem". The shop has to reply within 48 hours.

I'm a shop owner. What do I need?

A smartphone, your PAN and Aadhaar for KYC, and the list of colonies you deliver to. You can be live the same day.

My shop plan has ended. How do I renew?

In the app, open Shop & KYC and tap "Send renewal link to my email". The link brings you to our secure renewal page.
