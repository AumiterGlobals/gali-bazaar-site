How it works

No minimum app fee for customers. No waiting on hold. Just order and relax.

1

Pick your items

See products from shops that deliver to your address. Search by typing or just speak into the mic.

2

Choose a time slot

The shop accepts, packs your order and sends it with its own KYC-verified delivery person. Track it live on the map.

3

Check, then pay

Check every item at the door. Return anything you don't want and pay only for the rest — by cash or UPI.

Questions

Quick answers. Still stuck? Email us.

When can I download the app?

The Android app is launching on Google Play soon. Follow this page — the download button will go live here.

Is there a delivery charge?

Each shop sets its own delivery charge and "free above" amount. You see it clearly before you place the order.

How do I pay?

Cash or UPI at your door, after checking your items. You never pay inside the app.

What if an item is bad?

Return it at the door and don't pay for it. If you find a problem later, open the order and tap "Report a problem" — the shop has to reply within 48 hours.

I'm a shop owner — what do I need?

A smartphone, your PAN and Aadhaar for KYC, and the list of colonies you deliver to. You can be live the same day.

My shop plan has ended — how do I renew?

In the app, open Shop & KYC and tap "Send renewal link to my email". The link brings you to our secure renewal page.
