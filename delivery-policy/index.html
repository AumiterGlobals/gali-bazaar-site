Delivery Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 • Applies to the Gali Bazaar app and galibazaar.site

TermsPrivacyRefundsDeliveryDisclaimerContact
In short: Each shop delivers with its own delivery partners in the time slot you choose. Gali Bazaar does not deliver orders itself.

1Who delivers

Your order is packed and delivered by the shop or its own KYC-verified delivery partner. You can see their name, photo and phone number on the order.

2Where we deliver

Each shop chooses the localities and blocks it delivers to. You only see shops that deliver to your saved address.

3Delivery slots

You pick a slot (for example 5–7 PM, today or tomorrow) when you order. Each slot has a limited number of orders; full slots cannot be picked. The shop may decline an order it cannot fulfil.

4Delivery charges

Each shop sets its own delivery charge and minimum order. You see the exact amount before you place the order.

5At your door

Check every item before paying. Hand back anything expired, damaged or wrong and pay only for what you keep. Pay the delivery partner in cash or UPI.
