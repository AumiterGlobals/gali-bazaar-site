In short: Gali Bazaar connects you with shops in your gali. You pay the shop in cash or UPI at the door, shops pay us a small subscription (0% commission), and each shop is responsible for its products.

1About Gali Bazaar

Gali Bazaar is a platform that connects customers with local grocery, food-corner and stationery shops in their area. We do not sell, stock, pack or deliver any product ourselves — every order is between you and the shop.

2Your account

  • You sign in with your Google account; keep your name and delivery address correct.
  • One account per person. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Your phone number is never shown to shops.

3Orders & payment

  • Prices, stock and delivery slots are set by each shop.
  • Payment is cash or UPI on delivery, paid directly to the shop or its delivery partner.
  • You can cancel an order free of charge until the shop accepts it.

4For shop owners

  • One-time Aadhaar + PAN KYC is required for owners and every delivery partner.
  • 14-day free trial, then a paid plan purchased on galibazaar.site. 0% commission on orders.
  • If a plan is not renewed, the shop becomes inactive and is hidden from customers.
  • Shops are responsible for the quality, freshness and correct pricing of their products, and for their bills and taxes.

5Prohibited items

Tobacco, cigarettes, bidi, gutkha, pan masala, nicotine products, alcohol and any illegal item cannot be listed. Such listings are blocked automatically and repeat attempts lead to account suspension.

6Limitation of liability

Gali Bazaar only provides the platform and is not responsible for transactions, payment fraud or product quality between customers and shops. Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
