FAQs

How do I pay for my order?

Pay in cash or UPI to the delivery partner when your order arrives.

My item was damaged

Hand it back at the door, or open the order and tap Report an issue with a photo. The shop must reply within 48 hours.

How do I list my shop?

Install the app, choose Shop owner and finish KYC (PAN + Aadhaar). Your 14-day free trial starts once KYC is approved.

How do I renew my shop plan?

In the app open Account and tap Send renewal link to my email, then pay on galibazaar.site.

How do I install the APK?

Tap Download APK on the home page, open the file and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser once.
