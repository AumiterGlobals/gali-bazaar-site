Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 12 November 2026 • Applies to the Gali Bazaar app and galibazaar.site

TermsPrivacyRefundsDeliveryDisclaimerContact
In short: Customers pay the shop at the door, so there is nothing to refund online. Shop plans bought on galibazaar.site are not refundable, but failed or double payments are always returned.

1Customer orders

Cancel free until the shop accepts. Orders are paid in cash or UPI on delivery, so there is nothing to refund online. Wrong, damaged or missing items: hand them back at the door, or use Report an issue on the order — the shop replaces or refunds in cash.

2Shop subscriptions

Plans are bought on galibazaar.site. Once a plan is active it is not refundable; it stays valid till its end date.

3Failed or double payment

If money is deducted but the plan is not active, or you were charged twice, it is refunded to the same account in 5–7 working days. Write to support@galibazaar.site with your payment reference (UTR).

4Cancel auto-renewal

There is no auto-debit — you renew only when you choose to.
