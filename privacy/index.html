Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 • Applies to the Gali Bazaar app and galibazaar.site

TermsPrivacyRefundsDeliveryDisclaimerContact
In short: We collect only what is needed to show you nearby shops, deliver orders and verify shops. We never sell your data, and shops never see your phone number.

1What we collect

  • Everyone: name and email from Google sign-in, and the role you choose.
  • Customers: mobile number, delivery address, orders, chats with the shop, ratings and complaint photos.
  • Shop owners & delivery partners: shop details and KYC documents (PAN, last 4 digits of Aadhaar, PAN and masked-Aadhaar photos, live selfie). We never store the full Aadhaar number.
  • We do not collect contacts, SMS or call logs, and the app shows no third-party ads.

2How we use it

Only to run the app — show shops that deliver to your address, deliver orders, verify shops and delivery partners, send order updates and handle complaints. We never sell your data.

3Your phone number

Never shown to shops. Customers can see the delivery partner’s number to call about an order. KYC documents are visible only to Gali Bazaar admins.

4Service providers

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage (India region).
  • Google — sign-in, push notifications, and on-device text reading to check KYC photos.
  • Brevo — emails such as renewal links.

We may share data if the law or a court requires it.

5How long we keep data

  • Account and order data: while your account is active, and up to 90 days after deletion.
  • Bills, credit notes and payment records: as long as tax law requires (generally 8 years).
  • KYC photos: deleted within 90 days after the account is closed.

6Delete your data

In the app open Profile › Delete account, or email support@galibazaar.site from your sign-in Gmail. We erase your data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep. You can also ask for a copy of your data or raise a grievance with us; if unsatisfied you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
