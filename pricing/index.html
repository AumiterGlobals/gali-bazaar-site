Questions shop owners ask

Is there any commission on orders?

No. Gali Bazaar charges 0% commission — you only pay for your plan.

What happens after the free trial?

Your shop becomes inactive (hidden from customers) until you choose a plan. We email you a reminder a day before.

How do I pay for a plan?

On galibazaar.site by UPI — any UPI app or QR code. A GST tax invoice appears in your app and seller panel.

Is there an auto-debit?

No. You renew only when you choose to, using the link we email you.

Can I get a refund?

Plans are not refundable once active. Failed or double payments are refunded in 5–7 working days.

Do customers pay anything?

No — the app is always free for customers.
